## Design fixed in advance

Use local Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, all 30 library concepts, six non-topical everyday prompts (the first six bank prompts with no library alias/stem), one generation seed, and matched concept/prompt/seed pairs across conditions. Default research seed is zero. Each complete generation cell has n=180 answers and 30 concept clusters. Generation uses 32 tokens, temperature 0.8, top-p 0.95, answer scheduling, prefill injection and sixty saturating pushes. Absolute position zero is excluded. No failed/empty answer is silently dropped. Report its failure separately, and do not call a partial cell complete.

The primary configuration is injection layer 16, readout layer 20, alpha 0.45, others vectors, and immune off. The norm reference is the existing configured value 26.78, not a new measurement. Alpha-zero controls are matched. Runtime limits stay unchanged; larger research doses are isolated from the game. Dose grid: 0, 0.15, 0.3, 0.45, 0.6, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5. Injection grid: 4, 8, 9, 12, 13, 16, 18, 20. Readout grid: every integer layer from 12 through 22. These are one-factor sweeps around the primary configuration, with matched controls; they are not an unspecified full Cartesian search.

Primary concept appearance is whole-word keyword/alias hit. Stem and independent neutral-calibrated embedding hits are secondary, reported separately. Task kept uses the existing fixed lexical-anchor and repetition proxy; it is not a human relevance judgement. Blind readout ranks every concept, never just active factions. All rates report actual n and cluster count; unavailable metrics have no numerical estimate. No model-speed timings enter deterministic result tables.

All intervals use 2000 seeded bootstrap resamples of concept clusters, retaining every prompt/seed pair within a selected cluster. Differences are paired on concept, prompt and generation seed before resampling. Report two-sided percentile 95% intervals. Threshold decisions below use point estimates unless a lower bound or paired interval is explicitly required. Multiple exploratory grid comparisons are not multiplicity-corrected; they cannot replace the primary tests.

## Predictions and decision rules

1. **Concept appearance.** Alpha 0.45 increases keyword hit relative to alpha zero with immune off. Support requires the paired-difference interval's lower bound to exceed zero.
2. **Task cost and useful dose.** Task kept is non-increasing across the ordered alpha grid (ties allowed). A useful dose exists with keyword hit at least 0.2 and task kept at least 0.7. Both conditions are required; report the two parts separately, with all neighboring paired differences. A non-monotonic measured sequence fails the monotonic prediction rather than being repaired after inspection.
3. **Vector construction.** Others is at least as good as neutral mean-difference and raw concept mean, for both keyword hit and blind cosine top-1 at the primary dose. Support requires non-negative point differences and non-negative lower paired bounds against both alternatives on both endpoints; an interval crossing zero is inconclusive, not proof of equivalence.
4. **Blind reading.** Primary blind cosine top-1 is at least 0.9, and its paired difference from target-keyword detection has a lower bound above zero. Fitted v4 accuracy is additionally reported, without replacing this older-readout comparison after results are known.
5. **Defence.** At alpha 0.45, immune on reduces keyword hits by at least 80% relative to the matched immune-off rate, task kept is at least 0.95, and neutral alpha-zero any-alarm rate is at most 0.10. A zero immune-off hit denominator makes relative reduction undefined and this prediction inconclusive. Report paired rate differences and a cluster interval for relative reduction as well.
6. **Unseen examples.** On four unseen template families and disjoint seeds, the predefined full v4 model beats cosine with a paired top-1 interval lower bound above zero, and its own top-1 cluster lower bound exceeds 0.85. Test families, seeds and normalized text hashes cannot overlap training or validation. Use twelve test examples per concept (n=360 when complete). Finite-sample success never means certainty.
7. **Reading under defence and alarm separation.** Full v4 top-1 is at least 0.97 immune off and 0.95 immune on at the primary dose. On fresh alpha-zero answers, a confidence-based classifier alarm has a higher any-alarm rate than the reference projection/z-cosine alarm; report its paired difference and interval. The operational alarm keeps projection/z-cosine regardless of which prediction wins. Classifier labels, colours and reveal do not change its inputs.
8. **Injection location.** Average keyword hit over middle layers 9, 12, 13 and 16 exceeds that over late layers 18 and 20 at alpha 0.45, with a positive paired concept-cluster lower bound. Average within each answer key before resampling. Layers 4 and 8 are additional descriptive controls.

## Six vector constructions

All methods use the same self-generated contrast corpus and unit-normalize each nonzero direction. Both injection and cosine-readout directions use the named method at their respective layers. Others subtracts the unweighted mean of the other concept means. Neutral subtracts the common uninjected mean. Raw uses the concept mean without subtraction. Paired subtracts the mean of paired neutral generations made with matched sentence templates and generation seeds. Whitened applies a regularized neutral covariance inverse square root to the neutral mean-difference. Random uses independently seeded Gaussian directions per concept and layer, normalized to unit length as a negative control. No method is added or removed after inspecting results.

## Readout selection and alarm grids

V4 uses answer-mean hidden states at selected layers. Compare layer sets [16], [20], [22], [16,20], [16,20,22]; no whitening, diagonal standardization, or regularized full whitening; shrinkage LDA or ridge logistic classification; and training sizes 8, 16, 32, 48 and 66 examples per concept. The corpus contains 48 text examples and nine injected answers per immune setting per concept. Training subsets are deterministic nested prefixes, balanced by concept. Families and seeds are split before fitting. Validation alone chooses regularization within each cell. The primary full v4 model is fixed to [16,20,22], diagonal standardization, LDA, and the full training set; it is not whichever grid cell later scores highest on the test set. LDA shrinkages are 0.05, 0.2 and 0.5; logistic penalties are 0.01, 0.1 and 1. Ties prefer stronger regularization. Test data never selects these choices.

Readout and clamp calibration use at least sixty uninjected generations. Fresh alarm calibration uses a different seed and rejects text overlap. Clamp thresholds remain neutral projection percentile 97. For ROC/false-alarm analysis, vary alarm projection percentiles 95, 97, 99, 99.5 and 99.9; z thresholds 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6; consecutive-run lengths 3 and 8; and topical exemption off/on. Evaluate both immune settings using recorded per-token pre-clamp projections and windowed cosine z values, without substituting LDA logits. Add a matched topical alpha-zero set by explicitly naming each concept in the task, so exemption is tested rather than assumed to matter.

The alternative classifier alarm retains projection detection but substitutes maximum softmax confidence for the z branch. Its confidence threshold is percentile 99.5 of a separate uninjected validation set, not the test answers; its excess is standardized by that set's confidence standard deviation. Both alarms require attribution to the same concept for the configured run. Report detection, false alarms and true-concept attribution separately, with and without the prompt exemption. ROC axes and threshold-selection data must be labelled; no test-optimal threshold is called operational.

## Interpretation limits fixed before measurement

This design concerns one small model, a limited concept catalog, few prompts and one main generation seed. Keyword, embedding and task scores are proxies. Directions and the readout share a model family, and the contrast library is fitted on data whose representation it also reads. Held-out template/seed tests reduce one source of circularity but do not remove it. Determinism is scoped to the same CPU, packages, model files and numerical settings. Repeated deterministic observations do not create independent samples or evidence about other models.
